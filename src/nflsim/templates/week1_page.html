Week 1 Slate

Sixteen games, each simulated 20,000 times from opponent-adjusted 2025 efficiency, this year's quarterbacks, injuries and player status, coaching changes, venue and weather, blended with the opening market line. The blue bar starts on the picked team's side and shows that team's win probability; the grey marker is the market's probability for the same team with the vig removed, so a bar that reaches past the marker means the model likes the pick more than the book does. Reading the numbers

  • Confidence: High is 70 percent or better, Moderate 60 to 70, Lean 55 to 60, Coin flip under 55. A High pick still loses about three times in ten.
  • Fair moneyline is what the simulated probability implies. Market is the nflverse opening line, which predates most camp injuries.
  • "vs market" is the pick's model probability minus the market's vig-free probability. Positive means the model likes the pick more than the book does.
  • Injury and weather inputs come from web search summaries on Sept 4 and 5 and should be refreshed on game day.
