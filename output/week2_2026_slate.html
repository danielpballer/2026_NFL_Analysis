Reading the numbers

  • Confidence: High is 70 percent or better, Moderate 60 to 70, Lean 55 to 60, Coin flip under 55. A High pick still loses about three times in ten.
  • Fair moneyline is what the simulated probability implies. Market is the nflverse line at build time; compare with current prices before acting.
  • "vs market" is the pick's model probability minus the market's vig-free probability. Positive means the model likes the pick more than the book does.
  • Injury and weather inputs come from web search summaries and, once published, the official injury report; refresh on game day.

What changed after Week 1

  • Ratings now update in season: the preseason rating counts as nine games of evidence and each played game adds one, measured by opponent-adjusted EPA and margin. Week 1 moved no team more than 3.4 points.
  • Margin variance is re-estimated from 2023 to 2025 results (12.7 points around the line) with an early-season allowance that fades by Week 4.
  • The official nflverse injury report is merged with the researched list once it posts on Wednesday.
  • Unchanged: the 55 percent market weight (the blend matched the market in Week 1, the pure model did not) and home-field advantage (three-season data supports it).
